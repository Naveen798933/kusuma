==============================================================================
🎵 BACKGROUND MUSIC INSTRUCTIONS 🎵
==============================================================================

To add your romantic background music:

1. Copy your romantic audio track into this folder (`public/audio/`).
2. Name the file: `bgm.mp3`
3. In `src/config.ts`, you can customize:
   - `bgmTitle`: "Golden Hour" (or your favorite song name)
   - `bgmArtist`: "Our Song"

* NOTE: The web experience includes procedural Web Audio sound effects 
  (tap, match, level victory chime, celebration fanfare) that work 100% 
  out of the box even before you add an mp3 file!
==============================================================================
